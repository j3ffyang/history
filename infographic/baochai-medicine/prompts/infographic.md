## Style Guidelines

Historical scientific illustration on aged paper. Background is a warm parchment tone with faint foxing, fibre grain and one or two iron-gall ink spots in the margins. Primary ink is sepia brown, with faded red used only for the four chips and for tiny annotation marks. Line work is fine cross-hatching; shading is achieved by hatch density, never by gradients. Diagram elements carry small numbered tick marks like a specimen plate, but the numbers are decorative marks, not text. Typography mixes a clean serif for body lines with a slightly rougher handwritten hand for the short labels. Everything must look printed in one pass on one sheet — consistent ink weight, consistent paper texture, no drop shadows, no glow, no modern flat-vector look, no card UI.

1. 来源 — 下姑苏采买女孩子. For 元妃省亲, 贾蔷 was sent to buy girls and opera equipment in 姑苏; they lodged in 梨香院.
2. 名册 — two rows of six names. Row 1: 文官 宝官 玉官 龄官 芳官 藕官. Row 2: 菂（dì）官 葵官 豆官 艾官 茄官 蕊官. The book never lists all twelve in one place; these are the recoverable names. 菂 reads dì (lotus seed).
3. 行当 — 正旦芳官·小旦蕊官·小生藕官·大花面葵官·小花面豆官·老外艾官·老旦茄官. The role-types at chapter 58's dispersal.

5. 龄官 — 划"蔷"几千个·关在这牢坑里. In chapter 30 she scratched the character 蔷 thousands of times beneath the rose trellis; in chapter 36 she refused to sing and called the garden a "牢坑".
6. 遣散 — 老太妃薨·愿去的只有四五人. After a 老太妃 died, an imperial decree freed all household opera troupes; most girls would not go home.
7. 逐出与出家 — 美优伶斩情归水月. Chapter 77: 王夫人 expelled 晴雯, 芳官 and 四儿; then 芳官 followed 智通, and 蕊官 and 藕官 followed 圆心, to become nuns.
8. 脂批 — 跳出一个女优来. Marginal commentary on these girls, e.g. 庚辰本双行夹批 "四字奇想，写得纸上跳出一个女优来。"
